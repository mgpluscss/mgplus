Modal & Dialogs

Mg+ uses the native HTML5 <dialog> element (.mg-modal or .mg-dialog) for all modals, drawers, and bottom sheets.

Native dialogs render in the browser's Top Layer, guaranteeing they are never trapped by parent z-index, transform, or overflow: hidden stacking contexts. They include built-in focus trapping, native Escape key dismissal, ::backdrop blur styling, and zero-JS form closing via <form method="dialog">.

Default Modal (Centered Dialog)
